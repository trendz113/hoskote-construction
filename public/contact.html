Customer enquiry policy

  • We welcome genuine residential construction requirements: new house construction, complete end-to-end construction, construction on purchased plots and property inspections.
  • We build in Hoskote and mutually agreed nearby locations.
  • We do not offer free site visits or free personalised quotations.
  • Sending drawings, sketches or architectural plans alone is not a construction enquiry. Please start by telling us your requirements, location and expected construction timeline.

What happens after you contact us

  1. We review your requirements, location and timeline.
  2. If you would like your property visited, we explain the inspection terms.
  3. After the inspection we provide a construction assessment and quotation according to the agreed scope.
  4. No pressure. Take your time to decide.
