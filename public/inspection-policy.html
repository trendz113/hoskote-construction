Property Inspection & Quotation Policy

A proper site inspection takes real time, travel and expertise, and we want to give every serious customer that attention. So property visits are booked as inspections. The inspection terms, including any charges, how they are adjusted if you build with us, and cancellation terms, are shared with you in writing before you confirm an appointment.

Terms are shared in writing before you confirm an appointment.

How it works

  • Free: phone consultation, general construction information and a preliminary discussion about your requirements.
  • A physical property inspection needs your property location and basic details, and agreement to the inspection terms.
  • Our representative visits only after the inspection is confirmed and an appointment is scheduled.
  • The inspection includes a physical visit, site observations and photographs where permitted.
  • We then share a construction feasibility discussion and a property-specific quotation according to the agreed inspection scope.
  • This applies to bank auction properties, purchased properties, vacant plots and other property inspection requests.

What a physical inspection is not

  • It is not legal title verification. Please use a qualified lawyer for title and document checks.
  • It is not an auction valuation or auction due diligence.
  • It is not soil testing or a structural safety certification.
  • Other specialist services are not included unless separately agreed.

We assess the site for construction. The quotation covers the scope agreed with you and is not a promise of a final price outside that scope.

From enquiry to quotation

Five steps, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Tell us your requirements

    Share your location, plot or property details, what you want to build and your expected timeline.

  2. Request the inspection

    Choose a property inspection. We explain the inspection terms.

  3. We confirm and schedule

    Once the terms are agreed, we schedule the site visit with you.

  4. Inspection and assessment

    We inspect the property for construction and discuss the agreed scope with you.

  5. Quotation

    We provide a construction assessment and quotation according to the agreed scope.

Who this policy applies to

  • Bank auction properties
  • Purchased properties
  • Vacant plots
  • Other property inspection requests

Service area: Hoskote and mutually agreed nearby locations. See service areas.

What an inspection does not include

  • Legal title verification. Please consult a qualified lawyer for title and document checks.
  • Auction valuation. We do not value properties for auction or purchase.
  • Structural safety certification. Our physical inspection is an assessment for construction, not a certificate.

Customer enquiry policy

We welcome genuine residential construction requirements and want to give them proper attention.

We welcome

  • New residential house construction
  • Complete end-to-end construction
  • Construction on purchased plots
  • property inspections
  • Construction contracts in Hoskote and mutually agreed nearby locations

Please note

  • We do not offer free site visits.
  • We do not offer free personalised quotations.
  • Sending drawings, sketches or architectural plans alone is not a construction enquiry.
  • Please first explain your actual requirements, location and expected construction timeline.

The cost calculator gives an indicative figure only. It is not a quotation.

Common questions

Why is the property inspection not free?

  • An inspection takes our team’s time and travel. Booking it as a service lets us give serious customers proper attention and keeps our focus on projects that will go ahead.

What are the inspection terms?

  • The inspection terms, including any charges, how they are adjusted if you build with us, and cancellation terms, are shared with you in writing before you confirm an appointment.

Does the calculator give me a quotation?

  • No. It multiplies built-up area by a package rate for an indicative figure. Actual costs depend on specifications, site conditions, the selected package and additional requirements.

Can I visit your office instead?

  • Yes, by confirmed appointment, to understand packages, specifications, the process and completed projects. See office visits.
