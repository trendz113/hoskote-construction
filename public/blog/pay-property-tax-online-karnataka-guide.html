Why Hoskote uses a different portal than BBMP

Most property tax guides online are written for BBMP (Bengaluru city) properties, and they'll point you to the BBMP portal. That's the wrong place if your property is in Hoskote or any other Gram Panchayat area — properties outside BBMP and municipal limits pay tax to the local Gram Panchayat instead, through a completely different system.

In Karnataka, Gram Panchayat property tax is collected and managed digitally through two connected portals:

  • e-Swathu (eswathu.karnataka.gov.in) — the property tax and record portal for Gram Panchayat area properties
  • BSK — Bapuji Seva Kendra (bsk.karnataka.gov.in) — the rural citizen-services portal that connects to the same underlying Gram Panchayat tax system

Both portals draw from the same records, so you can use whichever one works more smoothly for you on a given day — if one is slow or has a technical issue, try the other.

This applies whether your property is vacant land or a built house. An empty plot is still taxable — in fact, unauthorised and previously-untaxed properties, including farmhouses and vacant sites, have increasingly been brought into the tax net under recent Gram Panchayat digitisation efforts. Not having received a bill in the past doesn't mean nothing is owed.

What you need before you start

Keep these details ready — most come straight off your previous year's tax receipt or challan, if you have one:

  • PID (Property Identification Number) or survey number
  • Owner name as it appears on record
  • District, taluk, and Gram Panchayat
  • Ward, block, and road details — usually printed on a previous challan; if you don't have an old receipt, your local Gram Panchayat office can help identify these
  • Khata number, if available

If you've never paid before and have no previous challan at all, this is where things get harder to self-serve — you may need to visit the Gram Panchayat office once to get your property correctly identified in the system before you can pay online going forward.

Step-by-step: paying online

  1. Go to e-Swathu or BSK. Visit eswathu.karnataka.gov.in, or bsk.karnataka.gov.in → Property Tax → Pay Property Tax.
  2. Select your location. Choose your district, taluk, and Gram Panchayat (for Hoskote-area properties, select the relevant GP under Hoskote taluk).
  3. Search your property. Enter your PID/survey number, or search by owner name if you don't have a PID yet.
  4. Review the demand shown. The portal displays the tax due for the current financial year, plus any pending arrears from previous years — check this against your last paid challan if you have one.
  5. Pay through the gateway. Complete payment via the portal's integrated payment gateway (UPI, net banking, or card).
  6. Download your receipt immediately. Don't skip this — the receipt is what you'll need later for Khata transfer, property sale, or a loan application.

Why pending years cost you more the longer you wait

If your property tax has been pending for multiple years, the amount due typically isn't just the sum of each year's tax — most local bodies apply penalty interest on overdue amounts, which compounds the longer the dues sit unpaid. A bill that would have been a modest amount if paid on time can grow substantially across 3-4 pending years once interest is added.

The earlier you clear pending dues, the less you pay in penalties on top of the actual tax. If you're unsure how many years are pending or what the current total looks like, checking the portal directly (or having someone check for you) is worth doing before assuming a bill from years ago is still the accurate number.

Why banks and buyers ask for this receipt

A recent property tax receipt is one of the standard documents banks request during a construction or home loan process, and it's equally important if you're planning to sell — buyers and their banks will want to see tax payments are current, not pending. It's also typically needed for Khata transfer. Clearing pending dues isn't just about avoiding penalties; it's about keeping your documents ready for whenever you need them, whether that's tomorrow or several years from now.

Quick FAQ

My property is in Hoskote — do I use BBMP's portal?
No. BBMP only covers Bengaluru city limits. Hoskote-area properties fall under Gram Panchayat jurisdiction and use e-Swathu or BSK instead.
Do I need to pay tax on a vacant plot, even if there's no building on it?
Yes — vacant land is taxable too, and Gram Panchayats have been actively bringing previously untaxed vacant and unauthorised properties into the system in recent years.
I don't have any previous tax receipt at all — what do I do?
Try searching by owner name on e-Swathu or BSK first. If your property isn't found, you'll likely need to visit your local Gram Panchayat office once to get it correctly recorded before you can pay online.
How much will penalty interest add if I've missed several years?
This varies by Gram Panchayat and how many years are pending — the portal will show your actual current demand once your property is located, which is the only reliable way to know the real number rather than estimating.
