Contractor Left Your House Half-Built in Hoskote? We Can Complete It.

If your contractor or mestri left the work in between, HoskoteConstruction can take over the pending construction on clean properties in Hoskote.

Pending construction work in Hoskote

A house stopped half-way is stressful, and every month of delay costs rent and money. We have the manpower to take over a project that another contractor or mestri has left, and we plan the work to finish on a schedule we give you in writing. Contact us with details of your project and we will tell you what is possible.

Only in Hoskote

For pending work we take projects only in Hoskote.

Clean property only

The property must have clear documents. We do not take bank, auction or disputed properties for this.

Any stage

Foundation, walls, roof slab, plastering, flooring or finishing: tell us where work stopped.

How we take over your project

1. Tell us about the project

Share your location in Hoskote, the stage reached, a few photos and what is pending.

2. We inspect the site

We visit, check the completed work and list what is pending and what needs correction.

3. You get a written quote

The price is based on how much of the project is already complete and what is left to build.

4. Written agreement and schedule

Scope, stage-wise payments and a schedule are agreed in writing before work restarts.

5. We finish the work

Our team and contractors complete the pending work, and we send you progress updates.

Contractor took money and left? Know your options

If your contractor or mestri took payment and left the work incomplete, you may be able to recover your money or hold him to the agreement. What is possible depends on your documents: the agreement or quotation, payment receipts or bank transfers, messages, photos and the work actually done.

When we take over your pending construction, we can introduce you to an independent advocate who can read your papers and advise you on your options, including a legal notice or a court case. Legal advice and any case are between you and the advocate. HoskoteConstruction is not a law firm and cannot promise the outcome of any legal action.

Keep every agreement, receipt, bank statement, message and photo safe. They matter most if you decide to take legal steps.

How we price pending work

The price depends on how much of your project is already complete. We inspect the site, assess the completed and pending work, and then give you a written quote. The scope, stage-wise payments and schedule are agreed in writing before we start.
