Free House Construction Agreement Generator

Starting your house construction? Get everything in writing before a single brick is laid — total cost, payment milestones, what happens if material prices change, and what happens if the builder delays or disappears with your money. Free for anyone to use, not just our clients — because a written agreement is the single biggest protection against being cheated.

Why this matters in Hoskote: we've personally seen and rebuilt houses for families whose earlier contractor took a large advance, did partial work, and vanished — or kept demanding "extra" money mid-construction with nothing on paper to hold them to the original quote. A signed agreement with milestones and a fixed-cost clause is what stops that. Don't hand over money on a verbal promise, however trustworthy the builder seems.

Step 1 · Enter Details

Material Price Protection Clause (built in automatically)

Once a Fixed Total Cost is agreed and signed, this generator adds a clause stating the builder bears any increase in material cost (cement, steel, sand, etc.) during construction — the client's payable amount does not change mid-project because prices went up. This is the exact protection against builders who quote low and then demand "extra" money once work has started.

Documents & Disclosures Provided Upfront (built in automatically)

The generated agreement also lists, as a Schedule, every document the client is entitled to receive before and during construction — the approved plan, cost break-up, payment milestone schedule, and material brand/quality specification — so nothing is verbal and nothing is a surprise later.

This tool generates a general-purpose template for common use. It is not a substitute for legal advice. For high-value contracts, please get the agreement reviewed by a local advocate and printed on the correct value stamp paper for your state, then notarised or registered as required.

Free Tool · For Loan Submission

Bank Loan Cost Estimate Generator

Banks won't sanction or disburse a construction loan on a verbal number. They need two things in writing: your Panchayat/BMRDA-approved building plan, and a certified estimate of construction cost with a stage-wise break-up that matches their disbursement schedule. This tool generates that estimate document — ready to submit alongside your approved plan.

Why banks ask for this: loan-to-value is calculated on the estimated construction cost (typically 75–80% of it), and disbursement happens in stages that match physical progress — foundation, plinth, each slab, brickwork, and finishing. Without a stage-wise cost estimate, the bank has no basis to release your next tranche on time.

Step 1 · Enter Details

BOQ-style Trade Break-up (built in automatically)

The estimate splits your total cost into standard Bill-of-Quantities work categories — earthwork, concrete, form work, steel, masonry, plastering, painting, doors & windows, flooring, electrification, plumbing — the format banks' technical valuers are most familiar with.

Applying for a specific loan amount?

Enter your target loan amount and your bank's typical funding percentage (usually 70–80% of declared project cost) — the tool checks whether your estimate's total actually supports that loan, and tells you how much genuine additional scope (interiors, compound wall, sump, etc.) you may need to include if it falls short. Never pad rates or quantities beyond real cost — that's misrepresentation to the lender, not scope planning.

This is a general-purpose cost estimate for loan submission, not a certified engineering valuation. Some banks require the estimate to be signed by a licensed civil engineer/architect — we can arrange this certification for you on request. Download our detailed BOQ estimate template (Word) for the full itemised version with Part B (Interiors).
