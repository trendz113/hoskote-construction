Estimate summary (indicative, not a quotation)

How this is worked out: built-up area per floor × floors × package rate = base construction cost. The range adds our typical one-time costs that are not in the rate: plan approval ₹20,000–₹80,000, temporary electricity ₹15,000–₹40,000, permanent BESCOM connection ₹15,000–₹45,000, borewell ₹60,000–₹1,20,000 and water sump ₹35,000–₹70,000. Compound wall, gate, modular kitchen (except Dream Home), interiors, landscaping and land are not counted. GST treatment and design fees are confirmed in your written quote. Your actual cost depends on the plan, site and specification.
