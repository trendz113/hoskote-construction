Book a Professional Property Inspection – Hoskote

Physical visit, site observations and a property-specific quotation for your house construction. Booked by appointment.

Why a property inspection matters

Every plot is different: access, levels, neighbouring structures, drainage and existing construction all affect how and what you can build. A physical visit lets us look at your actual property before we discuss construction, so the quotation you receive is for your site and not a guess.

What you receive

  • A physical visit to your property by our representative
  • Site observations, with photographs where permitted
  • A construction feasibility discussion
  • A property-specific construction quotation according to the agreed inspection scope

How the inspection is confirmed

A visit takes our team’s time and travel. The inspection terms are agreed up front, which confirms the appointment and keeps our time for customers who are serious about building. We schedule the visit only after the payment is verified.

How the inspection fee is adjusted

If you award the construction contract to Hoskote Construction, the inspection fee is adjusted against your selected construction package. If you decide not to proceed, the fee is not refundable, subject to applicable law and our published cancellation terms.

Not included in the inspection

  • Legal title verification or legal advice
  • Auction due diligence or auction valuation
  • Soil testing
  • Structural safety certification
  • Other specialist services

These are available only if separately agreed, and through qualified professionals where required.

How booking works

Six steps. Nothing is confirmed until your payment is verified.

  1. Step 1: Submit property details

    Share the property location, dimensions and what you plan to build.

  2. Step 2: Our team reviews the enquiry

    We check the details first. Incomplete or unrelated requests may not be taken forward.

  3. Step 3: Agree the inspection terms

    If we can proceed, we share the payment details with you directly.

  4. Step 4: Appointment is confirmed

    We confirm the visit only after the payment is verified on our side.

  5. Step 5: Physical site visit

    Our representative visits the property at the agreed time.

  6. Step 6: Findings and quotation

    We share the inspection findings and the quotation agreed for the inspection scope.

This website does not take payment and never shows a payment as received. A person on our team verifies each payment before a visit is confirmed.
