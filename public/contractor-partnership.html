Contractor Partnership Programme

We welcome experienced independent contractors and construction professionals who can execute residential construction projects.

How the programme works

A short, clear process so that expectations are agreed before any customer is involved.

  1. Submit your details

    Send your business and experience details using the form below.

  2. We review your profile

    Our team reviews the profile you submit.

  3. Opportunities are discussed

    Project opportunities may be discussed based on customer location and requirements.

  4. Terms are agreed with us first

    Construction structure, scope, specifications and payment terms must be discussed and agreed with Hoskote Construction before dealing with the customer.

  5. Work begins

    Work begins only after mutual agreement.

Ground rules

  • Contractors must not independently change agreed customer pricing, scope or payment commitments.
  • Project allocation is subject to availability and agreement.
  • Submitting this form does not guarantee work.

Looking for a directory listing instead?

  • This programme is separate from the public Contractor Directory. Registering here does not list your business publicly. For a public listing, see List Your Business.
