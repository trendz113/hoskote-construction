The Brief

A 30x40 plot is a common but tight footprint in Hoskote — the challenge for the Manjunath family was making a compact home feel open and modern, not cramped, while keeping the exterior low-maintenance for a family with limited time for upkeep.

What the Comfortable Home Package Included

  • M20 concrete + TMT Fe500D steel (JSW/Jindal)
  • Ultratech / ACC cement throughout
  • Standard vitrified floor tiles
  • Aluminium windows, flush doors
  • Ashirvad plumbing, Finolex wiring
  • Standard Anchor/Roma switches
  • Bathroom fittings — Cera standard range
  • Economy interior emulsion paint
  • Weekly WhatsApp photo updates
  • Written agreement + GST invoice

The Outcome

Delivered as a G+1 structure on the 30x40 plot, with a terrace utility room, a geometric blue-grey facade, vertical slit windows for cross-ventilation without sacrificing privacy, and a palm-shaded sit-out area.

How We Build — the Same Process for Every Project

Every project, including this one, follows the same sequence — a free site visit and soil check, a written agreement before any work starts, foundation and structure built with branded materials and daily supervision, and stage-wise payments released only after you've approved the work. Curing and supervision happen every single day, not on a drop-in schedule.

Typical cost range for a similarly specified project: roughly ₹28–32 lakh for construction, before land, approvals, and compound wall.
