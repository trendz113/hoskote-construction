The Brief

Beyond a family home, the Nagaraj family wanted their 30x50 plot to generate rental income from day one — ground-floor commercial space that could be leased to a shop, with two full residential floors above for the family to live in, all under one structurally sound building.

What the Dream Home Package Included

  • Marble or premium wooden flooring option
  • Modular kitchen fully included
  • Premium Legrand/Schneider switches
  • Designer exterior cladding / façade work
  • Smart home wiring pre-installed
  • Premium Jaquar/Kohler fittings
  • Daily updates + weekly video walkthroughs
  • Dedicated site supervisor assigned

The Outcome

Delivered as a G+3 structure on the 30x50 plot — ground floor fitted with commercial shutters ready for lease, two stone-clad residential floors above, and a terrace utility area. The mixed-use structure was engineered to carry commercial floor loads on the ground floor in addition to the residential floors above.

How We Build — the Same Process for Every Project

Every project, including this one, follows the same sequence — a free site visit and soil check, a written agreement before any work starts, foundation and structure built with branded materials and daily supervision, and stage-wise payments released only after you've approved the work. Curing and supervision happen every single day, not on a drop-in schedule.

Typical cost range for a similarly specified project: roughly ₹75–90 lakh for construction (higher than a purely residential G+3 due to commercial-grade shutters and ground-floor fit-out), before land, approvals, and compound wall. This is a range based on our published Dream Home package rate for a G+3 structure on a 30x50 plot — not this specific family's exact figure, since final cost depends on individual site conditions and choices made during construction.
