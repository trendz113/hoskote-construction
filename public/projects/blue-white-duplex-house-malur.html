About this project

What the owners wanted. A duplex of about 2,000 sqft needs a front elevation that looks confident from the road, stays easy to maintain, and still feels warm rather than heavy.

What we did. We kept a simple blue-and-white palette with wood accents and let a few details carry the design: a jaali terrace screen, a glass balcony railing, concealed facade lighting and a slatted gate that repeats the facade's diagonal pattern.

Result. Delivered as a 2,000 sqft duplex in Malur in 8 months, with a jaali terrace screen, glass balcony railing, warm facade lighting, covered car porch, wooden-slat gate and blue-based compound wall.

Built-up area is approximate. Photographs of this house at different construction stages are available on request. Your own rate, specification and schedule are confirmed in a written quote and agreement.
