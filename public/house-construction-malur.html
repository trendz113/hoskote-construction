Before you build on a Malur plot

Whoever you hire, get these three things checked first:

  • Which local body keeps your records. Your khata and property tax record sit with either a town municipal council or a gram panchayat, and the approval route follows from that.
  • Your layout and khata type. Check whether the plot is in an approved layout and what kind of khata it has.
  • Your documents match. The Encumbrance Certificate, sale deed and khata should tell the same story.

We check this during the free site visit. We look at your documents and tell you which approval route applies to your plot, so your request goes to the right office the first time.

Useful reading before you start: EC, e-Khata and registration, building approval fees, and updating your khata after you build.
