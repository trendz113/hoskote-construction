Experienced Labour Contractors & Maistris Required

Warehouse Construction Project – Hoskote, Karnataka

Hoskote Construction is inviting experienced maistris and labour contractors to submit quotations for a large warehouse construction project in Hoskote. We are looking for reliable teams capable of independently managing complete construction labour activities, including masonry, plastering, RCC, steel reinforcement and centring work.

Submit Your QuotationContact Us on WhatsApp

Project requirements

The quantities below are approximate and subject to joint site measurement and approved drawings.

A. Red brick masonry

  • Material: red clay bricks
  • Brick size: 9 × 4 × 3 inches
  • Wall thickness: 8 inches
  • Approximate quantity: 25,000 sq. ft.
  • Proper line, level and alignment
  • Masonry at different heights
  • Openings for doors and windows
  • Additional RCC and lintel work as per approved structural drawings

B. Wall plastering

  • Approximate quantity: 1,00,000 sq. ft.
  • Plaster thickness: 12 to 15 mm
  • Internal and external plastering
  • Surface preparation
  • Proper finishing and levelling
  • Corners and edges finishing

C. RCC and lintel work

  • RCC lintels
  • Horizontal RCC beams wherever specified
  • Steel reinforcement placement
  • Concrete pouring
  • Concrete vibration and finishing
  • Curing
  • All structural work follows approved structural drawings and engineer instructions

D. Steel reinforcement work

  • Steel cutting
  • Steel bending
  • Steel binding
  • Reinforcement placement
  • Beam and lintel reinforcement

E. Centring and shuttering

  • Beam and lintel centring
  • Shuttering installation
  • Props and supports arrangement
  • Alignment and levelling
  • De-shuttering
  • Cleaning and stacking

F. Bathroom-related work

  • Four bathrooms in the building layout
  • One ground-floor bathroom
  • Three front-office bathrooms
  • Additional quantities and finishing requirements will be confirmed at the site

Who can apply?

  • Experienced construction maistris
  • Red brick masonry contractors
  • Plastering contractors
  • RCC labour contractors
  • Steel reinforcement teams
  • Centring and shuttering contractors
  • Complete construction workforce management teams

Labour management responsibilities

  • Skilled masons
  • Helpers
  • Plastering workers
  • Steel workers
  • Centring workers
  • Daily attendance
  • Worker wages
  • Food and drinking water
  • Accommodation
  • Worker transportation
  • Daily supervision
  • Work coordination and execution

Preference will be given to teams with experience in warehouses, factories, commercial buildings and large construction projects. Food, accommodation and transportation for workers must be included in the labour contractor's quotation.

Material responsibility

Construction materials will be supplied by the customer as agreed. We do not assume that all reusable equipment is supplied. Your quotation must clearly state what you need and whether it is in your scope or expected from us:

  • Centring plates and plywood
  • Props and supports
  • Scaffolding
  • Tools and equipment
  • Safety equipment
  • Other reusable materials

Submit Your Complete Labour Quotation

Please give item-wise prices instead of a single unclear lump-sum quotation. Your quotation should include:

  1. Red brick masonry rate per sq. ft.
  2. Plastering rate per sq. ft.
  3. Steel fixing rate per kg
  4. RCC lintel and beam labour rate
  5. Centring and shuttering rate per sq. ft. of contact area
  6. Concrete placing rate per cubic metre
  7. Maistri or supervisor charges, if separate
  8. Total number of workers available
  9. Food, accommodation and transport arrangements
  10. Expected daily production
  11. Expected project duration
  12. Previous commercial construction experience
  13. Daily wage or weekly payment expectations
  14. Any exclusions or additional charges

Payment information

  • Weekly measurement and progress verification
  • Weekly running bills
  • Payment terms to be mutually agreed before commencement
  • Mobilisation advance subject to agreement

Final quantities are decided after joint site measurement. Submitting a quotation does not award the work. The selection of contractors will be communicated to you directly.
