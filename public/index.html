Common questions

What is the construction cost per square foot in Hoskote?▾

Our packages start at ₹1,800/sq ft (Comfortable), ₹2,000 (Better-Living) and ₹2,200 (Dream Home) for construction cost. The rate for your house is confirmed in a written quote after a site visit. See packages and specifications.

What is included in the quoted construction rate?▾

The rate covers the construction work listed in your chosen package. It does not include plan-approval fees, temporary or permanent electricity connections, borewell and sump, compound wall and gate, interiors and furniture, or land. A modular kitchen is included only in the Dream Home package. GST treatment and design fees are confirmed in your written quote. See additional costs.

How does the stage-wise payment system work?▾

Payments are tied to completed construction stages instead of one lump sum. The stages and amounts are written into your agreement, and each payment is due only after its stage is complete. The typical stage breakdown is in our FAQs.

How long does it take to construct a house?▾

It depends on the number of floors, the plan, approvals and weather. We discuss a realistic schedule at the site visit and write the milestones into your agreement. See the construction process for typical ranges.

Can I visit completed projects before choosing a contractor?▾

You can see our completed houses on the projects page, and we can share more photos from foundation to finish on request. Ask us on WhatsApp about visiting a completed house.

How can I book a site visit?▾

Call or WhatsApp +91 97428 01802, or send the form on this page. We will contact you to fix a time that suits you. The site visit is free.
