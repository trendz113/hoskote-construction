End-to-end residential construction

Hoskote Construction offers end-to-end residential construction, subject to the agreed package and contract scope. Here is the journey, and who is responsible for what.

  1. Share your requirements

    Tell us your location, plot, what you want to build and your timeline.

  2. Inspection and quotation

    We inspect the property and provide a quotation for the agreed scope. The fee is adjusted in full if you build with us.

  3. Written agreement

    Scope, rate and payment stages are written down before work starts. We help prepare plan-approval documentation.

  4. Construction and updates

    Work proceeds in stages, with progress updates and stage-wise payments.

  5. Handover

    We walk through the finished house with you and hand over the keys and documents.

Included in the package rate

  • The construction work listed in your chosen package, to the agreed specifications
  • Stage-wise progress updates during construction
  • Handover walk-through and documents
  • Help preparing plan-approval documentation

Budgeted separately

  • Plan-approval fees, temporary and permanent electricity connections
  • Borewell and water sump
  • Compound wall and gate, interiors and furniture, landscaping
  • Land, and legal title verification (we do not provide this as part of an inspection)
  • GST treatment and design fees are confirmed in your written agreement

Property Inspection & Quotation Policy

A proper quotation starts with seeing your plot. Property inspections are booked in advance and are by appointment. The inspection terms, and how they are adjusted if you build with us, are explained before you confirm anything.

How it works

  • Free: phone consultation, general construction information and a preliminary discussion about your requirements.
  • For a physical visit, share your property location and basic details first.
  • Our representative visits only after the inspection is confirmed with you and an appointment is scheduled.
  • The inspection includes a physical visit, site observations and photographs where permitted.
  • We then share a construction feasibility discussion and a property-specific quotation according to the agreed inspection scope.
  • If you build with us, the inspection fee is adjusted against your selected package.
  • This applies to bank auction properties, purchased properties, vacant plots and other property inspection requests.

What a physical inspection is not

  • It is not legal title verification. Please use a qualified lawyer for title and document checks.
  • It is not an auction valuation or auction due diligence.
  • It is not soil testing or a structural safety certification.
  • Other specialist services are not included unless separately agreed.

We assess the site for construction. The quotation covers the scope agreed with you and is not a promise of a final price outside that scope.
