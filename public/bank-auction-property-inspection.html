Bank Auction Property – Site Inspection and Construction Assessment

Share the auction details, we review them, and we confirm a visit only after the paid inspection advance is verified.

Thinking of building on a bank auction property?

We build houses and can visit a property to assess it for construction. Because each auction visit takes real time and travel, we follow one process for every bank auction enquiry.

How we handle bank auction enquiries

  1. We do not agree to an unpaid physical visit.
  2. Please share the auction or property details first (bank, notice or reference, address, auction date).
  3. Our team reviews that information.
  4. We explain the inspection scope and the ₹1,000 advance.
  5. We confirm the physical inspection only after the advance is paid and verified.
  6. If you later award the construction contract to Hoskote Construction, the ₹1,000 is adjusted against your selected package.

What the inspection covers

  • A physical visit and site observations, with photographs where permitted
  • A construction feasibility discussion
  • A construction assessment and quotation according to the agreed scope

Not included in the inspection

  • Legal title verification or legal advice
  • Auction due diligence or auction valuation
  • Soil testing
  • Structural safety certification
  • Other specialist services

These are available only if separately agreed, and through qualified professionals where required.

Please note: Hoskote Construction is a house construction company. We do not provide legal title verification, auction due diligence or legal advice. Please consult a qualified lawyer before you bid.

Request a bank auction property inspection

This is a request, not a booking. We review your details first, then share the payment details. The visit is confirmed only after we verify the ₹1,000 payment.

Thank you for contacting Hoskote Construction.

Our team will review your details and contact you. Please note that physical property inspection and detailed property-specific quotations require a paid inspection of ₹1,000. Your visit is not confirmed until payment is verified and an appointment is agreed.

We use your details only to respond to this request. We do not sell or publish them. No payment is taken on this website.

Process at a glance

  1. Step 1: Submit property details

    Share the property location, dimensions and what you plan to build.

  2. Step 2: Our team reviews the enquiry

    We check the details first. Incomplete or unrelated requests may not be taken forward.

  3. Step 3: Pay the ₹1,000 inspection advance

    If we can proceed, we share the payment details with you directly.

  4. Step 4: Appointment is confirmed

    We confirm the visit only after the payment is verified on our side.

  5. Step 5: Physical site visit

    Our representative visits the property at the agreed time.

  6. Step 6: Findings and quotation

    We share the inspection findings and the quotation agreed for the inspection scope.
